OUR STORY

One platform. Many campus possibilities.

Campus Connect is a student-focused digital platform created to make campus life more connected, organised and accessible.

Students often need different places to discover campus events, find useful opportunities, connect with others and discover products and services. Campus Connect brings these experiences together in one place.

Our goal is simple: make it easier for students to discover what is happening around them and become more involved in their campus community.
